Original stone walls, a timber roof and light from above.

Pagliaroom is a single-room house in the old town of Santo Stefano Quisquina. The ground floor is one open space for cooking and sitting, with a shower room. A mint-green spiral stair leads to the bed in the loft, under a skylight and the exposed timber roof. Outside, a small table and two chairs stand by the front door.

The project

  

    
From a street-level storeroom to a house for two.

    
The house was surveyed as a single ground-floor storeroom of 26.26 m² under a mono-pitch roof, 5.40 m high at the back and 4.15 m at the street. The project keeps the rubble stone walls and the arched openings, fits a kitchen, living area and shower room on the ground floor, and uses the height for a timber sleeping loft reached by a spiral stair.
